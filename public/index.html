CRYPTOGRAPHIC PRIVACY

Privacy Engine & Zero-Leakage Schemas

Five layers of verifiable cryptographic and statistical privacy with zero cross-silo raw data movement.

Opacus Per-Sample DP-SGD & Tight Rényi DP

Clips per-record gradients via Opacus GradSampleModule and composes Poisson-subsampled Gaussian Rényi orders α ∈ [1.25, 128], achieving formal ε_RDP ≤ 9.5 (δ = 10⁻⁵) alongside Warner's Randomized Response Local DP and Laplace MIA logit perturbation.

Drop-Resilient SecAgg+ & Homomorphic Pedersen

Pairs add +s_ij and -s_ij; when Byzantine silos are rejected, surviving peers cancel only dropped mask shares. Verifies SHA-256 bindings and RFC 3526 1536-bit discrete-log homomorphic commitments C = g^v h^r mod p plus optional 1024-bit Paillier HE.

UNSW-NB15 Schema (42 Features, 10 Classes)

Supports automatic real CSV ingestion and multi-class attribution across all 9 attack families plus Normal.

CICIDS2017 Official CICFlowMeter Schema (78 Features, 15 Classes)

Uses all 78 official CICFlowMeter headers and automatically normalizes real ISCX *.pcap_ISCX.csv exports.
